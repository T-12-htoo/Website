March 10, 2026 — Strong fiscal Q3 2026 results

Oracle reported Q3 FY26 results with strong cloud growth, including very high growth in cloud infrastructure (IaaS). Management also described AI and multicloud demand as a key growth driver.

March 2026 — Guidance confidence raised
